Driving lessons in Liverpool

Learning to drive on Liverpool roads

Learning in Liverpool means real conditions from the start: the 40km/h zones and pedestrian traffic around Liverpool Hospital and the Bigge Street precinct, multi-lane roundabouts, school zones, then high-speed merging on the Hume Highway, Cowpasture Road, Elizabeth Drive and Camden Valley Way. Every lesson runs on the streets, roundabouts and examiner routes you will actually face on assessment day.

Preparing for the Liverpool Service NSW driving test test

We prepare Liverpool learners for the two Service NSW centres in the region: Orange Grove Road at Warwick Farm, and Edmondson Park. Mock tests run under real exam conditions on the routes examiners use, including the immediate decisions right out of the testing centre car park.

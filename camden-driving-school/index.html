Driving lessons in Camden

Learning to drive on Camden roads

Camden asks a learner for two very different kinds of driving, and both are within a few minutes of each other. The quiet side is the residential grid in Camden South and the streets off Cawdor Road and Menangle Road, which are wide enough and calm enough to take a first lesson without pressure. The demanding side starts at the town centre: Argyle Street runs at 40km/h with angle parking, constant pedestrian movement and give-way decisions in close quarters, and John Street, Mitchell Street and the Onslow Park car parks add low-speed precision and parking practice. From there the roads open up. Camden Valley Way, the Camden Bypass and Narellan Road supply real speed management, lane choice and multi-lane intersections, and the school zones near Camden High School and Camden Public School force the speed changes assessors watch for. Lessons work up that ladder deliberately, so nothing on test day is the first time you have seen it. Camden sits in the Macarthur half of our coverage, taught alongside our Campbelltown driving school lessons.

Preparing for the Macarthur Service NSW driving test test

Most Camden learners book the Service NSW centre at Macarthur, a straight run out along Narellan Road, with Macquarie Fields the common alternative. Your lessons run on the roads around whichever centre you book, rehearsing the intersections, roundabouts, parking and observation habits assessors mark on the day. Learners on the northern side of town train on the same Narellan Road approach used for our Narellan driving school, a few minutes out along Camden Valley Way.
